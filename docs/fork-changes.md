# What this fork adds, in full

Moved here whole from the [README](../README.md) on 26 November 2026, so that the README stays short. Nothing was left out.

**27 November, evening: more pieces, each off by default.** `VICTORY_TABLE_VIEW_CACHE` (fxceless/victory-fork #1484: a table's texture views made once and copied), `VICTORY_GUARD_VIEWS` (avatarmanz: the guards' own record of which blocks are views, so the kernel is not asked), `VICTORY_CARD_ROOM_FOLLOW` (VI-BR-PORT: the room kept for render targets follows what they take, with no fixed number), `VICTORY_SUBMIT_LINE` (fxceless/victory-fork's port of VIctory-NVIDIA's submission line, the first step towards thin lists; nothing of it derived from XCL), `VICTORY_LIBRARY_TRIM_LOW_MB` (ours: the pipeline library's pages let go while pipelines still load, when the PC's available memory falls under that many MB), `VICTORY_OOM_RECLAIM` (ruff-lab: idle lists, spare scratch and snapshot pools emptied and the allocation tried once more on out-of-memory). With no switch: #1495, a translator table of 256 entries instead of 128, so a scalar destination above 127 no longer panics; shaders are translated anew once.

**27 November: pieces brought from other projects, each off by default.** Merged from the stacks prepared that day; with every variable unset the layer does what it did before. Measured so far: only the first (see [measurements](measurements.md)); the rest wait for their sessions.

- From edsuuu/VI-BR-PORT 8bb5142d (its base is avatarmanz's; nothing of its files derived from XCL): `VICTORY_HELD_LEVELS` (a placed texture's host copy starts at the first level its views show), `VICTORY_SEND_OWN`, `VICTORY_UNDER_WORDS`, `VICTORY_UNDER_WIDE` (copies), `VICTORY_WIDE_FILLS`, `VICTORY_REFRESH_AHEAD` (refreshes), `VICTORY_PAGE_PARTS`, `VICTORY_GUARD_REST`, `VICTORY_OPEN_ONE_VIEW` (the write watch and guards), `VICTORY_HEAP_PASS_ASIDE` (the heap pass on a thread of its own). Their `HEAP_SYNC_ASIDE` and `QUICK_MIRROR` were here already as `VICTORY_SYNC_BESIDE` and `VICTORY_HEAP_LOCKS`.
- From fxceless/victory-fork: #1450 `VICTORY_PIPELINE_COMPUTE_WAIT_MS` (a frame waits at most that long for compute pipelines), #1461 `VICTORY_SAMPLER_ORDER` (static samplers made in the same order at every start), #1468 `VICTORY_CARD_READ_COPY` (title copies that read what work in flight writes are done on the card; ours added: a later refresh of the destination keeps the copy on the processor, and the switch turns itself off without enhanced barriers), #1476 `VICTORY_CARD_SIDE` (small buffers and windows of title memory on the card; here capped at 90 % of the budget), #1424 `VICTORY_WIDE_READS`, #1427.
- From Belobug-AMD: the FidelityFX bridge (`VICTORY_FIDELITYFX_DIR`, a folder with AMD's own `amd_fidelityfx_upscaler_dx12.dll` from its FidelityFX SDK, which is not in this repository): the title's FSR calls go to the PC's FSR 3.1.
- Ours: `VICTORY_WINDOW_ICON` (an `.ico` of your own for the window); `VICTORY_LAZY_REMAP`'s copies read directly and its untouched pages count as clean (it ran at 1.3 fps before these fixes, see [measurements](measurements.md)).

The fork follows victory-fork and is merged up to its `e4245f3` (26 November 2026). The latest merge, of three commits up to `e4245f3`, brings the original's black-face texture fixes (see the table); its `9613092`'s diagnostics file, which lists offsets and instructions of two of the title's shaders, is left out. The merge before it, up to `58de9bf`, of two commits, changes the translator with no switch, so shaders are translated anew once: the values of half precision that the programs animating faces move through 16-bit buffer views keep their bits (fxceless/victory-fork#927: on NVIDIA they read as 0 and mouths stood still; no mouth has been seen from close here yet), and a sign extension is mended (the original project's #3124). It brings, off by default, `VICTORY_CLAMP_LEAN`, `VICTORY_CLAMP_WINDOW`, `VICTORY_OCCLUSION` and meters. Measured with the rest of that evening's merge, the ten switches of Status, a quiet machine, one session: 6.64 fps and a median frame of 144 ms over the manoeuvre, against 6.69 and 141 on the code before it. The merge before it, up to `ceb9e1f`, of two commits, turned on one thing and mended another: a compute dispatch of more than 65,535 groups is spread across z (`VICTORY_DISPATCH_SPREAD=0` goes back; the translator adds a prologue to the compute programs that read their group id, so shaders are translated anew once), and the BC7 rotation is applied only in modes 4 and 5. It brought, off by default and to be measured here one at a time: `VICTORY_SETTLE_ROOM=1` (twice the lists in flight and eight times the changed runs merged before flights are settled; its author measured 11.5 ms less of the preparing thread's work at equal draws), `VICTORY_PARALLEL_REFRESH=1` (a submission's texture checks run side by side on the helper threads; 8.7 and 9.4 ms less there), `VICTORY_NEAR_WRITES`, `VICTORY_SYNC_BESIDE` and `VICTORY_DENY_PIPELINES`. Measured on this machine with the ten switches of Status on, one session after a warm-up, with the DispatchX fix and the other changes in the table below on top: 6.24 fps and a median frame of 155 ms over the 280 s of the manoeuvre (95th percentile 205 ms), against 6.41 fps and 146 ms before this merge; in the 60 s of still room before the manoeuvre, 6.18 fps against 5.84. The layer's preparing fell from 87 to 82 ms a frame, but the card ran our lists 122 ms a frame where it ran them 107, and the thread that submits waited for it 54 ms where it waited 43 to 53. The spread was the cause. Turned off and on by turns of 20 s within one session, seventeen turns: 6.49 fps (median frame 146 ms) off and 6.09 (157 ms) on, slower in each of the eight pairs of turns, the card running our lists 108 against 122 ms a frame. About 930 indirect dispatches a frame went through the spread's signature, and none in that session asked for more than 27,936 groups. So in this fork indirect dispatches keep the signature of before until a record asks for more than 65,535 groups, and take the spread's from then on for the rest of the run (`VICTORY_DISPATCH_SPREAD=always` is the original's way). With that, one session: 6.69 fps and a median frame of 141 ms over the manoeuvre (95th percentile 200 ms), 6.33 fps in the still room, and no dispatch through the spread's signature. The merge before it, up to `f6a09d3`, of ten commits, changed two things with no switch, both in the translator, so every shader is translated anew once (a multiply-add kept as AMD's RDNA 2 does it, and typed views of 32-bit integers), and the box in the corner of the picture now shows six lines about loading (`VICTORY_FPS_COUNTER=fps` leaves the frame rate alone). It brought, off by default and to be measured here one at a time: `VICTORY_HEAP_WORKER=1` (a thread of the layer's own records the uploads of textures picked by index), `VICTORY_TABLE_STAGES=1`, `VICTORY_HEAP_LOCKS=1`, `VICTORY_BAKE_SOURCES=1`, `VICTORY_REAPER=1`, `VICTORY_TILES_SMALL_WHEN_FULL_MB` and `VICTORY_RETIRE_LEAN=1`. Measured on this machine with the ten switches of Status on, its shader cache warm but for 380 programs, one session: 1,794 frames, 6.41 fps, median frame 146 ms (95th percentile 218 ms), against 6.52 to 6.67 fps and 143 to 147 ms in five sessions before it; the private memory of the process peaked at 16.5 GB where it had at 17.9. The merge before it, up to `988aa41` (25 November 2026; 66 commits since `79cfdd9`), brought, on by default: changed bytes found 64 at a time by a mask (`VICTORY_CHANGED_RUNS=0` goes back to the walk byte by byte), a host pipeline for a draw into a render target of another format than its pipeline declares, a view of a texture the card wrote copied from it even if the list had already uploaded that view from title memory, constant buffer views capped at the 64 KiB the host takes, saved games that no longer fail on an older version that cannot be removed, and a report that says more when the card is lost. Its own fix for blending on integer render targets replaces the one ported here. Off by default, to be measured here one at a time: `VICTORY_CARD_BUFFERS_MIN_KB`, `VICTORY_CARD_HASH_WIDE=1`, `VICTORY_CARD_HASH_LEN=1`, `VICTORY_CARD_SYNC_BATCH=1`, `VICTORY_LOOKUP_CACHE=1`, `VICTORY_LIGHT_REFRESH=1`, `VICTORY_STEP_INDEX=1`, `VICTORY_FILLS_PLAIN=1`, `VICTORY_LIST_REUSE=1`, `VICTORY_HEAP_PASS_MARKED=1`, `VICTORY_HEAP_SYNC_CUT=1`, `VICTORY_GATHER_BY_COPY=1`, `VICTORY_TEXTURE_BACK=1` and `VICTORY_BVH_NO_HIT=1`. Measured on this machine with everything it brings at its default, same settings and manoeuvre as the pair in Status, one session, its shader cache cold: 1663 frames, 5.94 fps, median frame 165 ms (95th percentile 209 ms), against 1,678 frames, 5.99 fps and 164 ms before the merge. No change, as was to be expected with its frame-rate work behind switches. The original's own README, with what it measured on its own machine (an i7-14700KF and an RTX 4070 of 12 GB: 9 to 12 fps in rooms, with a set of switches that has not been measured here yet), is kept as `README.upstream.md`. Earlier merges brought, on by default, a frame-rate counter drawn in the corner of the picture (`VICTORY_FPS_COUNTER=0` removes it) and DirectStorage reads into physical pages that wait for the title's Submit. The changes below are what this fork adds on top. Original authors retain their credit and history.

| **DispatchX by thread counts** | The console's DispatchX carries flags that were passed over. The ray-tracing structure builder dispatches its stages that way, and taken as groups a stage of 64 threads a group ran 64 times the threads asked for and wrote megabytes past the end of its structure.
